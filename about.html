ABOUT

Clear numbers for ordinary household questions.

Current Ledger is a small independent information site focused on practical U.S. home electricity questions. The goal is simple: explain the calculation, state the assumptions, and give readers a useful way to replace those assumptions with their own numbers.

Our approach

Electricity estimates are easy to make look more precise than they really are. We therefore distinguish between rated wattage, estimated consumption, and measured use. When a primary source is available, we link to it.

What we do not do

  • We do not present one national electricity rate as everyone's actual rate.
  • We do not claim a calculation is a measurement.
  • We do not publish health, legal, or financial advice.
  • We do not use fake personal stories or testimonials.

Editorial standard

Guides are written for usefulness first. Information that can change—such as electricity prices—is dated and linked to a current source where practical.
